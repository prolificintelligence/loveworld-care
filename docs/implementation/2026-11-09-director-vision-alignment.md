# Director vision alignment and revised implementation sequence

Assessment: 9 November 2026. This plan incorporates the user's clarification that the native EMR is the clinical system already being delivered, and that equipment connections follow facility registration and approval.

## Conclusion

The principal product remains the **LW Health Intelligence Platform for the LoveWorld Health Services Directorate**: central registration, reporting, standards oversight, leadership intelligence and accountable intervention. The comprehensive native LW Care EMR and My LW Health Care experience support that purpose and the subsequently confirmed core scope. They must not displace the Directorate reporting outcome or make equipment integration a prerequisite for the reporting portal.

The implemented native EMR suffices as the platform's own clinical-system implementation for facilities choosing LW Care. No additional external EMR is required to use that route. This is an architectural and implementation-scope conclusion, not certification that every specialist workflow, protocol, interface, offline function or operational acceptance case is complete. The native EMR remains partial against the full suite until actual activation, adopted policies/content, staffing and observed qualification are completed.

## Sequence correction

The earlier next-phase recommendation placed external integration ahead of completing and adopting the Directorate reporting outcome. That ordering is corrected here. Existing engineering findings and historical evidence remain valid; this document supersedes their prospective sequencing.

1. **Complete the current Phase 6 cloud release and its recovery/evidence checks.** Keep its completion claims bounded to the delivered increment.
2. **Directorate operating foundation and reporting adoption.** Validate the actual Region → Zone roster and accountabilities; register facilities; exercise central affiliation; reconcile the Directorate's actual templates, reporting periods, metrics/case definitions, expected units and disclosure policies. Do not substitute fabricated criteria, source data or clinical zeros for missing adoption.
3. **Directorate reporting completion.** Prioritise the end-to-end path: facility/zone capture → submitted return → source validation and independent verification → permitted regional/global roll-up → independently released leadership dashboard → owned intervention/follow-up. Include missing/partial/late/corrected records, historical scope changes, renewal and unresolved exceptions.
4. **Qualified source automation from the native EMR.** Extend only adopted, testable mappings for activity, outcomes, illness/disease and other required returns. Retain controlled manual/CSV capture where a source is external or cannot yet be mapped. Preserve provenance and reconciliation so native and external/manual feeds do not double-count the same declared activity.
5. **Observed Directorate acceptance and controlled facility rollout.** Exercise actual officer and leadership journeys with the approved forms and real roles. Qualify privacy, accessible/low-bandwidth use, representative volume, reconciliation and recovery. Deployment and engineering tests alone do not establish institutional acceptance.
6. **Facility-specific equipment and counterpart connections, as approved demand arises.** An interface follows actual facility registration, verification/central approval, adopted service activation, accountable operator, approved counterpart/data mapping and secure credentials. Imaging/LIS/PACS/device connections are qualified for that facility and equipment. They are not a prerequisite for facility registration, Directorate reporting or the native EMR route.
7. **Remaining finance/sponsorship, governed assistance and full integrated acceptance.** Sequence these by adopted service needs without making optional ERP or AI a dependency for core reporting or care. Keep deferred offline installation and additional hosting visible, with cloud-compatible contracts/scripts continuing where useful.

## Delivery and activation boundaries

Real institutional inputs still govern operating activation: the official roster, mandated templates, definitions, affiliation criteria, reporting/disclosure policy, facility operators, authorised reviewers and receiving duty commitments. Their absence is not a request to procure a second EMR or connect unapproved equipment. Equipment/vendor credentials should be requested only when a registered and approved facility has selected that connection.

No baseline directive IDs or confirmed decisions are changed by this sequencing correction. The detailed release inventory and requirement evidence register continue to distinguish implemented software, executed engineering evidence and unexecuted clinical/institutional acceptance.
