**Latest implementation:** [Phase 4B institutional lifecycle and publication](2026-11-08-institutional-publication-release.md) adds reviewed hierarchy/site changes, affiliation correction/renewal/notices/appeal/reinstatement and approved content authoring/review/publication. The new `/governance` and `/learn` workspaces use the authoritative cloud API. Final focused checks passed 34 tests; production build and type checking passed. Live schema-6 and deployment evidence is being completed and will be recorded in the release report. The prior schema-5 recovery release and retained archive remain preserved.

**Scope decision:** offline care remains core; installing its facility-local runtime and deploying additional worker/scanner hosts are deferred beyond the current cloud stack. Cloud care still requires WAN access. Daily/manual processing is not continuous urgent escalation. Cloud recovery/reconciliation is delivered. The [recommended sequence](2026-11-08-implementation-review-and-next-phases.md) and [200-entry evidence register](2026-11-08-requirement-evidence-register.csv) retain the remaining work.

Its acceptance cases remain specification records; executed engineering tests and their results are recorded separately in the release evidence.

## Current delivered platform

The institutional release supplies real identity/MFA, scoped permissions, registration, inspection/affiliation decisions and public directory publication. The clinical release extends that live runtime with a persistent, encrypted EMR and staff workspace. The Directorate release adds actual reporting, independent verification, disclosure-controlled leadership evidence, protected safety review and accountable actions. The control release adds verified recovery without chart/account merging, independently reviewed country/operator/service activation evidence, accepted duty coverage, version-aware clinical overdue alerts, fenced durable jobs and staff restriction with personally accepted clinical transfer. Other institutional, protected-case and finance departure duties require their own review. The API has actual PostgreSQL transactions and provider integrations; there is no production fixture repository, simulated payment settlement or successful provider fallback.

The EMR includes patient registration and matching, provisional/local identifiers, demographic corrections, independently reviewed identity links, appointment requests and accepted commitments, arrivals and clinical ownership, triage, structured observations/problems/allergy assessments, clinical/nursing/specialty documents and intentional signing, addenda, treatment consent and separately scoped sharing grants, diagnostic orders/specimens/results and correction follow-up, formulary/prescribing/pharmacy/dispensing/MAR, clinical stock, physical beds/admissions/transfers/discharge, continuing-care programmes, procedure safety phases, transfusion witnessing/monitoring, referrals with actual receiving acceptance, and approved care tariffs/invoices/cash receipts with balanced ledger entries.

The Directorate module freezes unit/period obligations and metric versions, retains controlled import outcomes, prepares actual completed-encounter aggregate extracts, distinguishes nil/missing/partial/unknown values, records attestation and independent verification, and invalidates affected snapshots after corrections or late arrivals. Independent snapshot release applies adopted small-cell, contributing-unit, complementary and differencing controls to both display and CSV. Safety cases use actual accepted coverage, restricted reviewer notes, classifications, accepted handovers and required external receipt evidence. Actions retain source versions, named owners/fallbacks, resources, evidence review and observed outcome limitations; daily reconciliation records overdue exceptions and separate acknowledgements.

Each workflow remains bounded by actual facility activation, current professional authority and applicable policy. A clinical governor can issue another activated staff member's privileges; a Directorate administrator cannot browse clinical charts by seniority. No real facility readiness, professional registration, prescribing content, vaccine schedule or tariff has been invented to populate the application.

Current recovery deployment/test evidence is in [the cloud recovery release](2026-11-08-cloud-recovery-release.md); the preceding [control release](2026-11-08-control-release.md) retains its own evidence. Earlier [Directorate](2026-11-07-directorate-release.md), [EMR](2026-11-05-emr-release.md) and [institutional](2026-11-05-cloud-release.md) releases remain recorded separately. Clinical custody decisions are in [ADR-0008](../adr/0008-cloud-emr-authority-and-record-custody.md), reporting/disclosure in [ADR-0009](../adr/0009-directorate-reporting-disclosure-and-review.md), and recovery/jobs/activation/deferred hosting in [ADR-0010](../adr/0010-cloud-controls-recovery-and-deferred-hosting.md).

The recovery increment adds an independent operator-held archive key, verified row/file inventories and restored-key/clinical-lineage checks. Operator-run backup and isolated restoration are actual recorded actions. Automatic backup scheduling, independent off-account protection, source/provider configuration recovery, adopted RPO/RTO, representative load and authorised live failover remain separate work. No restoration overwrites the active cloud database.

## Cross-cutting specifications and operating dependencies

- **SPEC-003/016:** authority is enforced by the backend using stored account, facility staff membership, independent professional privilege, patient/encounter, service and confidentiality. Secure sessions, MFA, encrypted clinical content, minimal department views and protected access history are implemented. Verified recovery, fresh-factor restriction and independently reviewed activation packages are added. Independent operational security/privacy review and penetration testing remain.
- **SPEC-004/006/009/010/018/019:** typed commands, explicit missingness, version checks, immutable attestations/corrections, separate actual clinical events and transactionally owned obligations are implemented. Native EMR and reporting routes are explicit implementation extensions under `/v1/clinical/{facility_id}` and `/v1/reporting`; they do not silently redefine the archived capture schemas. Country-specific forms, licensed terminology and complete specialty content remain adoption work.
- **SPEC-011/015:** clinical and reporting transactions retain durable event/audit/outbox/receipt data. The authenticated daily job also records unique overdue reporting/safety/action exceptions; fallback acknowledgement is distinct from accepted ownership or external notification. Real database concurrency and replay checks are release evidence. The control release adds indexed clinical due work, accepted coverage, encrypted jobs, fenced claims, bounded retries/provider reconciliation and attempt receipts; daily/manual processing is deployed. The recovery release adds real encrypted archives and isolated database/file/key restoration with aggregate reconciliation. Continuous clinical escalation, capacity/on-call, independently held copies and representative-load/witnessed recovery remain unqualified. Vercel Hobby's daily cron is not presented as a clinical escalation scheduler.
- **SPEC-012/020 and ENG-007:** native permission-scoped source downloads preserve versions; they do not claim a certified FHIR, LIS/PACS/device or legacy-EMR migration interface. Those integrations require actual counterpart systems and conformance/cutover work.
- **SPEC-017 and OPS-006:** offline care remains core scope. The project lead defers deployments outside the current cloud stack, including facility-local installation; compatible engineering and deployment scripts may continue. This release requires WAN access to the cloud authority. Facility runtime provisioning, bounded offline identity/reference packages, node trust, fenced failover, semantic synchronisation and outage/restore qualification remain outstanding. Browser caching does not constitute committed offline care.
- **SPEC-021 and GOV-002/003/004/008:** live use depends on actual country/custodian authority, facility operating scope, a nominated clinical governor and professional verification. Clinical adoption, retention rules, specialty packages and legal/privacy approvals are not inferred from a successful deployment.
