## Current delivered platform

The institutional release supplies real identity/MFA, scoped permissions, registration, inspection/affiliation decisions and public directory publication. The clinical release extends that live runtime with a persistent, encrypted EMR and staff workspace. The Directorate release adds actual reporting, independent verification, disclosure-controlled leadership evidence, protected safety review and accountable actions. The API has actual PostgreSQL transactions and provider integrations; there is no production fixture repository, simulated payment settlement or successful provider fallback.

The EMR includes patient registration and matching, provisional/local identifiers, demographic corrections, independently reviewed identity links, appointment requests and accepted commitments, arrivals and clinical ownership, triage, structured observations/problems/allergy assessments, clinical/nursing/specialty documents and intentional signing, addenda, treatment consent and separately scoped sharing grants, diagnostic orders/specimens/results and correction follow-up, formulary/prescribing/pharmacy/dispensing/MAR, clinical stock, physical beds/admissions/transfers/discharge, continuing-care programmes, procedure safety phases, transfusion witnessing/monitoring, referrals with actual receiving acceptance, and approved care tariffs/invoices/cash receipts with balanced ledger entries.

The Directorate module freezes unit/period obligations and metric versions, retains controlled import outcomes, prepares actual completed-encounter aggregate extracts, distinguishes nil/missing/partial/unknown values, records attestation and independent verification, and invalidates affected snapshots after corrections or late arrivals. Independent snapshot release applies adopted small-cell, contributing-unit, complementary and differencing controls to both display and CSV. Safety cases use actual accepted coverage, restricted reviewer notes, classifications, accepted handovers and required external receipt evidence. Actions retain source versions, named owners/fallbacks, resources, evidence review and observed outcome limitations; daily reconciliation records overdue exceptions and separate acknowledgements.

Each workflow remains bounded by actual facility activation, current professional authority and applicable policy. A clinical governor can issue another activated staff member's privileges; a Directorate administrator cannot browse clinical charts by seniority. No real facility readiness, professional registration, prescribing content, vaccine schedule or tariff has been invented to populate the application.

Directorate deployment/test evidence is in [the current release record](2026-11-07-directorate-release.md). Earlier EMR evidence is maintained in [the EMR release record](2026-11-05-emr-release.md). The prior institutional release is recorded in [the original cloud release evidence](2026-11-05-cloud-release.md). Clinical custody decisions are in [ADR-0008](../adr/0008-cloud-emr-authority-and-record-custody.md); reporting, disclosure and accountable review decisions are in [ADR-0009](../adr/0009-directorate-reporting-disclosure-and-review.md).

## Cross-cutting specifications and operating dependencies

- **SPEC-003/016:** authority is enforced by the backend using stored account, facility staff membership, independent professional privilege, patient/encounter, service and confidentiality. Secure sessions, MFA, encrypted clinical content, minimal department views and protected access history are implemented. Recovery, operational security review and penetration testing remain.
- **SPEC-004/006/009/010/018/019:** typed commands, explicit missingness, version checks, immutable attestations/corrections, separate actual clinical events and transactionally owned obligations are implemented. Native EMR and reporting routes are explicit implementation extensions under `/v1/clinical/{facility_id}` and `/v1/reporting`; they do not silently redefine the archived capture schemas. Country-specific forms, licensed terminology and complete specialty content remain adoption work.
- **SPEC-011/015:** clinical and reporting transactions retain durable event/audit/outbox/receipt data. The authenticated daily job also records unique overdue reporting/safety/action exceptions; fallback acknowledgement is distinct from accepted ownership or external notification. Real database concurrency and replay checks are release evidence. Full clinical scheduling/escalation services, capacity tests, observability/on-call, backup/restore drills and operational service levels remain unqualified. Vercel Hobby's daily cron is not presented as a clinical escalation scheduler.
- **SPEC-012/020 and ENG-007:** native permission-scoped source downloads preserve versions; they do not claim a certified FHIR, LIS/PACS/device or legacy-EMR migration interface. Those integrations require actual counterpart systems and conformance/cutover work.
- **SPEC-017 and OPS-006:** the project lead explicitly deferred offline installation. This release requires WAN access to the cloud authority. There is no browser outbox pretending to commit clinical care offline. Facility runtime provisioning, bounded offline identity/reference packages, node trust, fenced failover, synchronisation conflict handling and outage/restore qualification remain outstanding.
- **SPEC-021 and GOV-002/003/004/008:** live use depends on actual country/custodian authority, facility operating scope, a nominated clinical governor and professional verification. Clinical adoption, retention rules, specialty packages and legal/privacy approvals are not inferred from a successful deployment.
