# Accepted delivery-team accountability

`LWH-EXT-DELIVERY-TEAM-001` adds a distinct institutional delivery-team identity. An account is its named lead, not its team ID. Current scoped facility administrators record the actual name, responsibility, authority/source reference and proposed existing lead. Only that lead, with current facility-administrator authority, can personally accept the exact proposal version. Creation alone establishes no acceptance, staffing, ministry mandate, professional competency or operating approval.

Team proposals, acceptance, revisions and retirement are encrypted immutable journal snapshots. They reuse existing schema-9 storage; no migration, table, host or dependency is required. Each command checks current scope, intent, idempotency and version under the existing institutional transaction serialization. Team lists are scoped and paged; version-paged history retains the actual actor, reason and prior snapshots without ciphertext/storage internals.

A corrective-plan proposal may explicitly select `owner_team_id` and `owner_team_version`. Both are required together. The referenced team must belong to the actual case facility, be currently accepted, and have the current assigned finding owner/respondent as its personally accepted lead. Independent exact-plan agreement remains mandatory. The protected plan retains the full accepted team snapshot. Current team acceptance and authority are rechecked at agreement, response and independent resolution. A revised, retired or reassigned team cannot silently carry pending work forward: propose and independently agree fresh work after actual acceptance.

Completed independently verified work retains its original accepted team version after later revision or retirement. A later positive recommendation/decision checks that historical accepted snapshot, original response/resolution facts, current evidence and independence. Team retirement cannot erase completed authorship or transfer it to a successor. Actual case handover and actual team-lead acceptance are separate steps; neither substitutes for the other.

Existing individually accountable plans remain supported. Omitted/null optional team fields are excluded from canonical request hashing to retain old receipt identity. Switching pending work to individual accountability requires an explicit new proposal, independent agreement and response; there is no automatic fallback or historical backfill. Original findings/deadlines/observations and response histories remain retained. Initial SCREENING ordering and file/scanner exclusion are unchanged.

This is a bounded FRM-010 owner-team reference extension. It records an actual team and an accepted accountable lead; it does not implement a complete multi-member roster, professional privileges, duty coverage, team governance/adoption policy or notifications. Generic tagged capture/missingness, safe initial criterion-to-finding linkage, full notice/appeal/offline semantics and actual-role/device acceptance remain open. No production teams, facilities or approvals are seeded for engineering qualification. Synthetic CLEAN metadata qualifies no malware scan, file, capacity target or operating decision.

Rollback requires a separately qualified schema-9-compatible artifact that understands accepted team/plan bindings and preserves newer journal/response/care/finance history. Do not restore old business data, remove schema markers or deploy an older workflow that bypasses these bindings.
