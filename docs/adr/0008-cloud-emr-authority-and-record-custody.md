# ADR-0008: Authoritative cloud EMR and clinical record custody

Date: 5 November 2026. Implements the clinical increment alongside ADR-0007. The project lead subsequently deferred the offline installation and requested testing and live cloud deployment without installing Playwright. Facility-local operation and synchronisation remain in the programme scope; they are not supplied by this cloud release.

The deployed institutional API is extended by `platform/clinical`; the existing process-local Care repositories are not reused. Clinical commands run against persistent PostgreSQL tables in `health_platform`. Migration 2 is additive and installs clinical sites, privileges, records, memberships, immutable events and audit, encrypted operation receipts, physical beds, stock lots and movements, and the balanced care ledger. It neither seeds example care nor changes the legacy application's schemas.

Clinical governors can record independently evidenced facility activation and another professional's bounded service privileges. PLATFORM_ADMIN, registry, standards and executive authority do not imply clinical chart access. A facility staff grant, active account, current independent clinical privileges, service activation and applicable treating relationship are checked by the API. Restricted records require an additional sensitive-data privilege. Diagnostic, pharmacy, registration and finance access is constrained to their work. An invitation alone cannot confer clinical signing or prescribing privileges.

Patient content and clinical operation responses are encrypted with AES-GCM before database storage. Site-scoped keyed hashes support exact-token patient matching without plaintext name indexes. Identifiers, actor, patient and encounter remain separate. A provisional identity can be issued without fabricating a birth date or examination. Reviewed identity links preserve two original charts; they are reversible crosswalks, not automatic merges. No medical vocabulary, licensed catalogue, clinical threshold, dose or price is invented or seeded.

Each clinical mutation checks its patient context, expected version, authority node/epoch, state and current permissions. The transaction commits the current result, encrypted historical event, protected audit, outbox reference, operation receipt and locally owned bed/stock/ledger effects together. PostgreSQL transaction locks and unique effect identities prevent conflicting occupancy, overspending stock and duplicate administration occurrences. Retrying a request returns the encrypted original receipt only after current access checks. Disclosure retries also revalidate the sharing grant and source restrictions.

Events retain actual and recorded times, author, applicable policy and professional privilege evidence, source content/version and a previous-event hash. Ed25519 signatures attest server-observed authenticated author intent; this is not a claim of a jurisdiction-specific qualified electronic signature. Signed note bodies cannot be updated or deleted through the API. A PostgreSQL trigger also rejects direct changes to a signed note, and source records cannot be deleted. Corrections and entered-in-error restrictions are separate attributed records. Event, audit, stock movement and financial ledger histories reject UPDATE and DELETE.

Treatment consent and sharing authorisation are separate. Sharing binds patient, exact recipient, purpose, selected source records, decision authority and expiry. A downloadable source package preserves versions and provenance; preparation does not claim remote delivery or recipient acceptance. Revocation prevents future grant-based downloads, including cached operation replays. It does not erase a previously lawfully retained clinical copy. The native package is not represented as a qualified FHIR interface.

Orders, specimen collection/receipt/analysis, result release, clinician acknowledgement, clinical response and actual communication are distinct actions. Corrections retain previous reports and create new review obligations. Pending result tasks survive encounter completion and discharge. An unresolved result task cannot be closed merely because a message was sent. Prescribing, pharmacy verification, actual dispensing and actual administration/omission are separate records. Medication safety checking is explicitly unavailable; the adopted clinician/pharmacist manual process must be recorded. No AI service receives clinical data in this increment.

The workspace provides real capture and commands for general, nursing, maternal/newborn/child, chronic, mental-health, rehabilitation, anaesthesia and recovery documentation. Structured observations retain source, units and explicit missingness. This does not supply adopted specialty algorithms, vaccine schedules, growth interpretation, device interfaces or a complete country operating package. Physical procedures and transfusions require actual clinical evidence; a planned operation cannot become performed through a scheduling action, and a named independent transfusion witness must personally attest the start.

The care-finance increment records approved tariffs, charges against actual fulfilled services, invoices and actual cash receipts. Each financial event posts balanced immutable ledger legs. Electronic settlement, payer claims, adjustments/refunds and the optional enterprise modules are not implied by a cash receipt. Clinical priority and discharge authority remain separate from payment.

Browser clinical content is not stored in localStorage or an offline cache. Session verification hides the workspace without destroying an unsaved form; changing patient/facility clears the active form context, and stale patient responses are fenced. Mutation failures preserve entered work and retry identity. The deployed browser requires the cloud authority to be reachable. This is not a facility-offline qualification or an independent bedside medication writer.

Rollback restores the previous Vercel deployments while retaining migration 2 and all clinical/financial history. Do not drop clinical tables or restore competing database writers as a rollback. Subsequent migrations must remain forward-compatible and retain original signatures, keys and source versions. Clinical activation requires actual nominated governance, operating evidence and bounded professional privileges; successful deployment does not manufacture these approvals.
