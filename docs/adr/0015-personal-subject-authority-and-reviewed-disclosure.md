# Personal subject authority and reviewed disclosure

Date: 9 November 2026. Implementation decision for LWH-PRD-023, LWH-SPEC-003/004/009 and representative authority LWH-FRM-022. Runtime contracts extend the baseline through the versioned `/v1/personal` boundary; the controlled authored suite is unchanged.

Accounts and patients remain separate. Public enrolment creates an inactive account, an expiring private email invitation and no staff grants. Existing password/MFA activation and controlled account recovery are reused. Repeating enrolment never resets an existing account or changes its staff authority. The public response does not identify account existence; queued delivery is distinct from a provider receipt.

A person requests self or representative access at an activated facility. An independent, currently privileged records professional resolves the confirmed patient, checks actual evidence and applicable policy, and records action-specific permissions, expiry and reassessment. The reviewer cannot be the account holder or submitter. One active self account is allowed for a facility patient identity. A representative cannot delegate onward. A verified self account with SHARE may nominate an already activated representative account, but independent review is still required.

Grants bind the reviewed patient version. Corrected demographics, provisional identity, expiry, reassessment or revocation stops future personal access until review. Administrative seniority, ministry membership, staff privileges and family labels establish no personal permission. Each HTTP request and idempotent replay rechecks current authority. Consequential commands use expected versions and encrypted durable receipts. Personal grants, requests and preferences retain append-only, hashed event history; messages, releases, withdrawals and audit are protected from update/delete in PostgreSQL.

Care is released separately to each verified permission by a currently privileged treating professional. Only signed/verified sources of the appropriate kind are eligible; a diagnostic result needs recorded clinical review. This increment withholds restricted sources. Patient-readable summaries are authored and attested by the professional and retain source/version and release provenance. They are not unrestricted copies of the staff chart. A source version/status/confidentiality change withholds an old summary. Signed amendments, corrected results and entered-in-error markers withdraw old releases atomically; retained pre-portal corrections are also checked when releasing or viewing information. Access history is subject-scoped.

Receiving services require a real activated facility, distinct current primary/fallback CARE professionals, an adopted response commitment and personal acceptance by both recipients. A submitted request has a durable named owner and update target, with no inferred clinical acceptance or new encounter. The owner personally accepts, responds and closes. Handover retains the old owner until the successor accepts. If an owner loses eligibility, the named current fallback may personally accept responsibility; this act is audited. Request revocation hides the patient's portal view but preserves accepted clinical work and its staff owner. An overdue open request remains an owned service exception; there is no claim of continuous urgent escalation on daily/manual infrastructure.

Request messages distinguish patient/representative reports from human care-team responses. Self-reports do not become verified clinical findings or medication adherence. This release includes in-app follow-up preferences, not external health reminders or an AI companion. Core work is independent of AI.

The personal interface clears visible care, conversations and drafts when the subject changes or a session needs verification; late responses are discarded by context generation. Pending operation keys remain scoped to their original subject/request in tab memory, so retries retain their original receipt without crossing subjects. No personal records are cached for WAN-offline clinical use. The staff interface requires independent professional authority and isolates review drafts by request.

Schema 8 adds ten personal tables and five immutable-history protections. Archives include these tables only for schema 8; retained schema-5/6/7 shapes exclude them and remain supported. Restore verification checks personal history hashes and current authority against recorded events. Production qualification, actual migration/recovery results, operating dependencies and remaining acceptance are recorded in the Phase 6 release report. These design decisions do not adopt local legal rules or clinical release policies.
