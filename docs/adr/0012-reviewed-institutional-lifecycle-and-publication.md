# ADR-0012: Reviewed institutional changes and approved publication

Date: 8 November 2026. Status: implemented; live qualification is recorded separately in the release evidence.

## Context

The requested Phase 4B increment follows the suite's Director-first authority, Region → Zone reporting spine, central affiliation decisions and separate identity, statutory, affiliation, publication and readiness axes. Primary owners are LWH-PRD-001/002/003/010, with SPEC-003/005/008/009/010/015/016 and OPS-001/002/003. The archived suite remains unchanged. Native commands/events use extension `LWH-EXT-INSTITUTIONAL-001`; they do not rename controlled APIs, forms or acceptance cases.

## Decisions

1. Use additive schema 6 with 18 institutional tables. Ten new history tables prohibit UPDATE/DELETE at PostgreSQL, bringing protected history tables to 48. Source files, findings responses, decisions, notices, editorial revisions, reviews and receipt events remain attributable and encrypted where sensitive. Existing clinical signatures and history remain intact.
2. Require a proposal and a distinct current reviewer for material site changes, duplicate links, closure and zone transfers. Platform administrators govern hierarchy; registry stewards govern sites. Use expected source versions, an exclusive pending source key and transactionally encrypted idempotency receipts. Cancellation is explicit before effect; reversal after effect requires a new reviewed proposal.
3. Record effective relationship facts and their original baseline. Period allocation resolves the relationship at the reporting period start. Existing return scopes/cohorts and published snapshots retain their recorded versions. Prospective changes become effective on the first relevant request at or after their approved time, before authority checks or new work. A bounded backlog fails closed. This is not a continuously running scheduler.
4. Closure/duplicate linkage withdraws listing and new intake but preserves existing charts, clinical tasks, identifiers and signed care. Duplicate linkage requires same-site evidence and retains both identities; it does not merge patients. A currently qualified clinical custodian personally accepts the continuity plan. Lost custodian authority at effect creates a restricted continuity-review exception. This is not automatic accepted transfer of every individual care task.
5. Renewal, noncompliance, appeal and reinstatement are new cases, retaining a frozen criteria snapshot, original decision head and prior notices/reasons. Separate competent inspection, reviewer recommendation and central decision. Positive recognition needs current criteria/evidence/authority. Opening a case never extends expiry. A stale case cannot overwrite a subsequent decision. Appeal review excludes prior participants.
6. Finding owners submit responses; independent reviewers resolve or return them. Ordinary adverse decisions require the response period to elapse and actual acknowledgement or separately evidenced human service of the notice. An urgent protective exception needs actual adopted authority and explicit continuity custody. Portal availability, acknowledgement and actual delivery are distinct events. Assignment changes remain offers until the named successor accepts.
7. Editorial versions freeze provenance, author, audience, language, jurisdiction, purpose, sources/licence, support links and validity/review dates. A qualified reviewer and distinct publisher approve the exact revision. Clinical governors review health education/guidance; appointed policy owners review rights/ministry/faith material. Administrative seniority confers neither authority. No policy wording, medical content or licence is inferred or seeded.
8. Public routes return only current explicitly public versions. Staff content remains private. Clinical guidance requires explicit matching jurisdiction before body/receipt. Review-due, expired or withdrawn bodies are unavailable as current text. A replacement needs a meaningful reviewed correction summary, retains old history and marks affected portal deliveries with the successor version. Delivery, read, acknowledgement, self-reported understanding, feedback and health outcomes stay distinct.
9. Distribution in this increment is a portal delivery to actual permitted staff accounts. It sends no external notice/email. Patient preferences, translation qualification, document rendering, additional delivery channels and service outcome evaluation remain future work.
10. Keep the real private-store/scanner path. File upload fails closed without a configured scanner. Engineering recovery fixtures explicitly marked TEST_NOT_SCANNED prove byte restoration only. No malware qualification is claimed.

## Migration, recovery and rollback

Deploy the compatible API while production remains schema 5; new institutional routes return 503. After CI, exact artifact and isolated Neon checks, apply migration 6 and then deploy the web workspace. Migration seeds no domain content or authority. Existing provider settings, application encryption key, independent recovery escrow and the retained schema-5 archive remain unchanged.

Continuity archives record their actual schema version. Restore accepts the exact schema-5 or schema-6 table inventory, installs the corresponding 38 or 48 history protections and restores both evidence file families to temporary private paths. A rehearsal never overwrites production. The old retained archive is explicitly rehearsed after migration; a new schema-6 archive is separately captured and rehearsed.

For an incident, pause new institutional commands and deploy a reviewed compatible fix. Keep approved effects, new records and the additive schema; do not drop tables, remove constraints, replace keys or overwrite live data to simulate rollback. Already approved timed effects continue to enforce restrictions. Reversal of a material change or decision uses a new attributable reviewed action.

## Consequences and remaining acceptance

The cloud stack can operate the implemented institutional and publication journeys without AI or a new host. Real roster/calendar/criteria/content/licensing and authority appointments remain institutional adoption inputs. Broader reporting stewardship, source/template reconciliation, full case/exit variants, automatic renewal notifications, read-only public-document variants and witnessed UAT remain outstanding. Offline care remains core; this cloud release requires WAN access, while facility-local and additional scanner/worker deployments remain deferred.
