# LW Health Intelligence Platform — Documentation Index

**Complete authored documentation suite · Version 1.0.0 · 5 November 2026**

Prepared by Prolific Intelligence for Pastor Emmanuel Patrick and the Health Services Directorate.

LW Health Intelligence Platform is the umbrella. LW Care is its care-delivery capability; My LW Health Care is the working patient app name. The suite follows the Director’s institutional mandate and the decisions captured in the interactive session.

The suite contains **78 controlled Markdown documents**, including **25 PRDs and 21 shared specifications**; **51 forms and 355 tagged fields**; and supporting schemas, interface contracts, traceability and implementation guidance.

The core scope includes comprehensive facility EMR across the four agreed facility types, both existing-EMR integration and migration routes, purpose-based record sharing, Region → Zone oversight, central Directorate affiliation decisions and full local clinical operation during WAN loss. Accounting, procurement, HR and payroll are optional. Directorate core funding is the leading proposal; pilot countries are unselected.

Policies and country/service activation dependencies are explicitly labelled. The 111 implementation acceptance cases are specified and **NOT_RUN**. Document/schema checks have passed; this is not a clinical approval or implementation certification.
