# EMR adoption, role training and facility activation

## Purpose, trigger and staffing

Use for introducing native LW EMR, integrating an existing system, changing clinical scope or onboarding a new role/team. The result is competent staffed operation with adopted country/service evidence and dependable support.

The accountable owner is **Facility implementation and clinical training leads**. Assign named primary/deputy contacts and staffed coverage at activation. An absence does not remove open responsibility; the adopted handover/escalation remains explicit. Operate only within the applicable country/facility policy, professional privileges and purpose-scoped access.

## Procedure

| Step | Responsible role | Action and decision | Evidence / handoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| LWH-OPS-007-S001 | Implementation owner | Select integration/migration route and confirm country/operator/custody/funding responsibilities. | Scoped adoption plan and evidence gaps |
| LWH-OPS-007-S002 | Facility/department leads | Map actual workflows, roles, professional privileges, services, devices and patient flow. | Local workflow/source inventory; no assumed capability |
| LWH-OPS-007-S003 | Data/interface team | Qualify mappings/import/cutover and resolve active identity/clinical/finance issues. | Dry-run reconciliation and rollback/custody plan |
| LWH-OPS-007-S004 | Technical/local owner | Provision local runtime, strong identity, valid packages, printing, backups and department adapters. | Qualified capacity/outage/restore evidence |
| LWH-OPS-007-S005 | Training lead/supervisors | Train each role on normal, wrong-subject, unavailable service, handover, privacy and downtime tasks. | Observed competence, remedial support and deputies |
| LWH-OPS-007-S006 | Clinical/product users | Perform integrated UAT with realistic synthetic cases across every enabled service. | Actual usability/safety observations and unresolved blockers |
| LWH-OPS-007-S007 | Activation owners | Review exact country/facility/service dossier, coverage, support, funding and acceptance evidence. | Scoped authorised decision/version; no blanket global enable |
| LWH-OPS-007-S008 | Post-launch service team | Support limited approved cohort, reconcile actual work, monitor burden and review expansion. | Daily exceptions, weekly review and controlled change |

## Exceptions and continuity

Staff attendance alone is not competence. Optional ERP and AI are not prerequisites for core care. Missing local department/credential/data authority blocks that activation rather than producing a fictitious service. Migration rollback preserves new clinical work. New languages, specialties and country rules reopen relevant training/review.

## Records, supervision and review

Keep role competence records, privilege/coverage evidence, configuration/content versions, test/drill results, patient communication materials, support contacts and adoption feedback. Measure repeated entry, staff workload, missed handovers, source errors and patient experience as well as system usage.

Preserve actual event and recorded times, actor, source/version, decision, authority and successor acceptance. External delivery does not prove review or fulfilment. Signed/history/ledger records are corrected by addendum/reversal rather than silent overwrite. Patient information is shared only through the permitted purpose and channel; general support/notifications use neutral minimum context.

## Readiness and acceptance

Every actual role/deputy completes relevant tasks with correct permissions and can recover from a failure. All enabled clinical workflows pass local WAN-outage qualification, and operating owners can execute incident/restore and reconciliation procedures before live activation.

Train actual roles and deputies through a complete synthetic successful and failed journey. Record observed effort, missed handoffs, source gaps and downtime recovery. Review recurrent exceptions and update the relevant policy/forms/training through controlled change. A runbook is not operationally ready until named staff can execute it with the actual configured contacts and systems.
