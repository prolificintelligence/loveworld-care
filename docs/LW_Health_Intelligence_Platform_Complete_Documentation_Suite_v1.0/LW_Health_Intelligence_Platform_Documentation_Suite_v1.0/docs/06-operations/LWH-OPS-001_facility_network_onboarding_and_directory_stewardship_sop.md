# Facility/network onboarding and directory stewardship

## Purpose, trigger and staffing

Use when an institution/site joins, changes ownership/location/services, changes administrative reporting assignment or closes. The result is a verified, correctly scoped site and accurate public service information with accepted ongoing responsibilities.

The accountable owner is **Network registry lead**. Assign named primary/deputy contacts and staffed coverage at activation. An absence does not remove open responsibility; the adopted handover/escalation remains explicit. Operate only within the applicable country/facility policy, professional privileges and purpose-scoped access.

## Procedure

| Step | Responsible role | Action and decision | Evidence / handoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| LWH-OPS-001-S001 | Facility administrator | Capture operator/site identity, ownership, location, contacts and declared services using FRM-001. | Source/evidence references; progressive draft |
| LWH-OPS-001-S002 | Registry steward | Search local/external IDs and candidate duplicates; distinguish one owner/multiple sites. | Reviewed identity/link decision; disputed cases assigned |
| LWH-OPS-001-S003 | Directorate operations | Assign validated region/zone and reporting responsibilities with effective dates. | Primary assignment, account/delegation and obligation records |
| LWH-OPS-001-S004 | Network/clinical lead | Verify declared services, responsible teams, professional privileges and actual readiness. | Separate capability/readiness/licence/affiliation status |
| LWH-OPS-001-S005 | Standards secretariat | Open the applicable affiliation case and coordinate evidence/inspection. | Case ownership; central decision through OPS-003 |
| LWH-OPS-001-S006 | Registry publication officer | Publish approved public fields only after eligibility checks; show verification/recognition meaning. | Public projection/version; no private evidence |
| LWH-OPS-001-S007 | Facility/network owner | Set refresh/support cadence and route material changes/closure into review. | Readiness expiry, correction queue and continuity plan |

## Exceptions and continuity

Missing evidence remains unverified. No GPS permits a sourced manual location. Cross-zone specialist services use service links without duplicate site creation. During WAN loss capture proposals and local readiness with actual authority; central recognition/publication remains pending. Closure/withdrawal stops new matching as appropriate and transfers active care/records obligations without deletion.

## Records, supervision and review

Retain site/operator IDs, evidence, reviewed duplicate links, hierarchy dates, public/private field decisions, service readiness and actual change history. Monitor stale profiles, duplicates and unsuitable routing. Registry, standards and clinical owners review their separate facts.

Preserve actual event and recorded times, actor, source/version, decision, authority and successor acceptance. External delivery does not prove review or fulfilment. Signed/history/ledger records are corrected by addendum/reversal rather than silent overwrite. Patient information is shared only through the permitted purpose and channel; general support/notifications use neutral minimum context.

## Readiness and acceptance

Qualify one owner/two sites, similar names/different sites, multi-country region, historical zone transfer, expired readiness and closure with active care. Verify public APIs/exports exclude private evidence.

Train actual roles and deputies through a complete synthetic successful and failed journey. Record observed effort, missed handoffs, source gaps and downtime recovery. Review recurrent exceptions and update the relevant policy/forms/training through controlled change. A runbook is not operationally ready until named staff can execute it with the actual configured contacts and systems.
