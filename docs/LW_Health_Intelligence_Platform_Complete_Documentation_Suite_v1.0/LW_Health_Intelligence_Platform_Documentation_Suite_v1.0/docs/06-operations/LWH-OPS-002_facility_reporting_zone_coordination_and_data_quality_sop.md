# Facility reporting, regional/zonal coordination and data quality

## Purpose, trigger and staffing

Use for scheduled returns, continuous event/source capture, missing-unit follow-up and corrections. The result is a reviewed, reproducible information snapshot with visible coverage and owned gaps.

The accountable owner is **Health information lead**. Assign named primary/deputy contacts and staffed coverage at activation. An absence does not remove open responsibility; the adopted handover/escalation remains explicit. Operate only within the applicable country/facility policy, professional privileges and purpose-scoped access.

## Procedure

| Step | Responsible role | Action and decision | Evidence / handoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| LWH-OPS-002-S001 | Information administrator | Open adopted obligations for expected facilities/zones and local periods; freeze hierarchy/version references. | Expected-unit register and deadlines |
| LWH-OPS-002-S002 | Facility officer/interface | Prepare approved EMR-derived totals or direct/bulk return; inspect source/gaps and explicit nil/partial/unknown. | FRM-003/004, source extract/version and missingness |
| LWH-OPS-002-S003 | Facility accountable officer | Check unit/period/definitions, attest and submit. | Local commit/central receipt distinguished |
| LWH-OPS-002-S004 | Validation/data steward | Run schema/logical/code/unit/duplicate/temporal checks; account for every input. | Blocking/warning/quarantine outcomes and owner |
| LWH-OPS-002-S005 | Scoped independent reviewer | Compare permitted evidence, verify or return specific correction; record explained override. | FRM-012, method/scope and actual reviewer |
| LWH-OPS-002-S006 | Zone coordinator | Follow missing units and separately capture community/context stream; assign support. | Coverage exceptions, overlap risk and accepted actions |
| LWH-OPS-002-S007 | Regional coordinator | Review zone gaps/comparability and route cross-zone needs to Directorate. | Regional roll-up with unchanged definitions |
| LWH-OPS-002-S008 | Analyst/information owner | Release eligible snapshot with coverage/lineage; link concerns to interventions. | Versioned metrics and disclosure-approved views |
| LWH-OPS-002-S009 | Originator/reviewer | Process corrections through new source versions and recompute/notify affected outputs. | Prior/current difference and retained history |

## Exceptions and continuity

A failed connector is unavailable data, not nil. Late submissions remain late with outage explanation. A verified partial return remains partial. Do not sum an EMR extract and attested return or add community notifications to unique facility patients. Offline attestations queue; central review waits for actual receipt. Urgent occurrences use OPS-004/clinical escalation instead of waiting for month-end.

## Records, supervision and review

Proposed calendar is monthly attested returns with continuous event capture and weekly exception review, subject to adopted indicator rules. Retain obligations, templates, source/check/review versions, corrections, snapshots and follow-up. Monitor completeness, timeliness, verification lag and reporting effort.

Preserve actual event and recorded times, actor, source/version, decision, authority and successor acceptance. External delivery does not prove review or fulfilment. Signed/history/ledger records are corrected by addendum/reversal rather than silent overwrite. Patient information is shared only through the permitted purpose and channel; general support/notifications use neutral minimum context.

## Readiness and acceptance

Reproduce a past period after hierarchy change, reconcile overlapping feeds, distinguish zero/missing/partial/late and trace a correction through dashboards. Actual reporting staff must complete the process with supplied source templates reconciled.

Train actual roles and deputies through a complete synthetic successful and failed journey. Record observed effort, missed handoffs, source gaps and downtime recovery. Review recurrent exceptions and update the relevant policy/forms/training through controlled change. A runbook is not operationally ready until named staff can execute it with the actual configured contacts and systems.
