# Clinic, referral, education and intervention fulfilment

## Purpose, trigger and staffing

Use when a community clinic/service is activated, a patient needs another service, a reviewed health concern needs action or approved education is distributed. The result is actual accepted delivery and reviewed follow-through.

The accountable owner is **Regional/zonal health operations and clinical network leads**. Assign named primary/deputy contacts and staffed coverage at activation. An absence does not remove open responsibility; the adopted handover/escalation remains explicit. Operate only within the applicable country/facility policy, professional privileges and purpose-scoped access.

## Procedure

| Step | Responsible role | Action and decision | Evidence / handoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| LWH-OPS-004-S001 | Clinic/network lead | Confirm service scope, current team/readiness, operating window and actual receiving/escalation routes. | FRM-015 and tested contacts |
| LWH-OPS-004-S002 | Referring clinician | Assess need/suitability; prepare authorised summary and retain responsibility. | FRM-016 and record-sharing authority |
| LWH-OPS-004-S003 | Receiving team/coordinator | Confirm capability/capacity and actual acceptance; coordinate transport/contact feasibility. | Recipient/time/conditions; decline path visible |
| LWH-OPS-004-S004 | Sender/receiver | Record actual departure/receipt and accepted clinical handover with outstanding work. | Transfer evidence; results/follow-up owners |
| LWH-OPS-004-S005 | Programme owner | Create evidence-linked intervention with intended result, resources, owner acceptance and review. | FRM-006; unfunded/blocked state retained |
| LWH-OPS-004-S006 | Editorial/policy owner | Approve applicable sourced education/rights/policy content and permitted audience. | FRM-013/version; source/clinical/ministry purposes distinct |
| LWH-OPS-004-S007 | Delivery owner | Perform local support/education/service action and record actual outcome evidence. | Delivery versus engagement/clinical outcome distinguished |
| LWH-OPS-004-S008 | Reviewer | Review referral outcome/intervention proof and comparable follow-up measures; close/reopen. | FRM-014 and unresolved obligations retained |

## Exceptions and continuity

No suitable/available receiver leaves sender responsibility and an escalation/alternative. Directory contacts are not ambulance dispatch. Offline requests remain queued unless actual phone/manual acceptance is documented. Community reports are reviewed signals. Unclear policy wording from audio cannot become a clinical restriction. Content withdrawal/correction reaches cached/affected recipients under policy.

## Records, supervision and review

Track clinic readiness, acceptance/receipt/outcome, action resources, delivery evidence, content versions and review limitations. Coordinators own operational support; qualified teams own clinical decisions. Use neutral notifications and purpose-limited data.

Preserve actual event and recorded times, actor, source/version, decision, authority and successor acceptance. External delivery does not prove review or fulfilment. Signed/history/ledger records are corrected by addendum/reversal rather than silent overwrite. Patient information is shared only through the permitted purpose and channel; general support/notifications use neutral minimum context.

## Readiness and acceptance

Demonstrate unavailable receiver, accepted specialised transfer, missing critical follow-up, unfunded intervention, expired content and corrected education. Review actual patient access and operational burden alongside programme activity.

Train actual roles and deputies through a complete synthetic successful and failed journey. Record observed effort, missed handoffs, source gaps and downtime recovery. Review recurrent exceptions and update the relevant policy/forms/training through controlled change. A runbook is not operationally ready until named staff can execute it with the actual configured contacts and systems.
