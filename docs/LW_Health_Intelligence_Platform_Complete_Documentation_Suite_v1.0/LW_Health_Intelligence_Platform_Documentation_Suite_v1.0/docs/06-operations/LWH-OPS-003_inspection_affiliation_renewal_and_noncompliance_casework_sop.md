# Inspection, affiliation, renewal and noncompliance casework

## Purpose, trigger and staffing

Use for new recognition, renewal, changed services, findings, protective restrictions and review. Final affiliation decisions are centrally recorded by the Directorate under adopted authority.

The accountable owner is **Directorate standards secretariat**. Assign named primary/deputy contacts and staffed coverage at activation. An absence does not remove open responsibility; the adopted handover/escalation remains explicit. Operate only within the applicable country/facility policy, professional privileges and purpose-scoped access.

## Procedure

| Step | Responsible role | Action and decision | Evidence / handoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| LWH-OPS-003-S001 | Secretariat | Select applicable adopted criteria/version, screen identity/evidence and assign accountable case officer. | Application, evidence validity and checklist |
| LWH-OPS-003-S002 | Assigned inspector | Declare conflicts, confirm scope/method and conduct purpose-appropriate visit/review. | FRM-008 observations/evidence distinct from declarations |
| LWH-OPS-003-S003 | Applicant/correction owner | Receive findings, respond and propose/complete corrective work with evidence. | FRM-010, due/review and actual completion proof |
| LWH-OPS-003-S004 | Independent standards reviewer | Examine dossier, unresolved findings and response; recommend reasoned disposition. | Recommendation, limits and conflict review |
| LWH-OPS-003-S005 | Central Directorate decider | Approve/condition/reject/renew/restrict under adopted terms; record reason, scope and dates. | FRM-007/011 and authenticated decision |
| LWH-OPS-003-S006 | Secretariat/publication owner | Issue notice/review route; create conditions/renewal work; update eligible directory separately. | Actual notices, tasks, publication status and expiry |
| LWH-OPS-003-S007 | Independent review authority | Handle challenge, proportionality, corrected evidence and reinstatement. | Original decision retained with reviewed outcome |
| LWH-OPS-003-S008 | Network/clinical custodian | Arrange accepted care/referral/records continuity for closures or restrictions. | No abandoned active patient/task or erased history |

## Exceptions and continuity

Do not infer government accreditation or clinical licence from affiliation. Incomplete/self-declared evidence remains labelled. AI may organise the dossier but cannot decide. Offline inspection capture is allowed; final central decision stays pending until central authority is available. Fees do not substitute for evidence. Known urgent risk uses adopted protective action with review rather than unreasoned automatic sanctions.

## Records, supervision and review

Retain criteria/evidence/visit/reviewer/decision versions, conflicts, notices, responses, corrective and appeal history. Proposed annual calendar and 90/30/7-day reminders require Directorate adoption. Review overdue conditions and recurrent failures as improvement needs, not only approval throughput.

Preserve actual event and recorded times, actor, source/version, decision, authority and successor acceptance. External delivery does not prove review or fulfilment. Signed/history/ledger records are corrected by addendum/reversal rather than silent overwrite. Patient information is shared only through the permitted purpose and channel; general support/notifications use neutral minimum context.

## Readiness and acceptance

Run applicant → inspection → central decision plus incomplete evidence, conflict, expiry, adverse notice, review and service continuity. Confirm inspector/region accounts cannot make final affiliation decisions.

Train actual roles and deputies through a complete synthetic successful and failed journey. Record observed effort, missed handoffs, source gaps and downtime recovery. Review recurrent exceptions and update the relevant policy/forms/training through controlled change. A runbook is not operationally ready until named staff can execute it with the actual configured contacts and systems.
