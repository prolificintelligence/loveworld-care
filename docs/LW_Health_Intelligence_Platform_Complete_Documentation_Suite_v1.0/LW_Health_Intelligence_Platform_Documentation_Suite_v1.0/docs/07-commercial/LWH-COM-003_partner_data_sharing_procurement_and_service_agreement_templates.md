# Participation, service and data agreement templates

## Use and execution variables

These substantive templates are drafting schedules for qualified legal/commercial review. Populate actual legal parties/addresses, authorised signatories, countries/governing law, service/site scope, adopted policy versions, amounts/currency, dates, support/notice contacts, liability/insurance and dispute provisions before execution. None is fabricated from research or the recording. Template completion and signing are separate from this authored documentation baseline.

Use only applicable schedules and reconcile conflicting terms. Institutional participation, clinical service, data processing, sponsorship, technical supply and optional ERP can have different parties/responsibilities. Attach the corresponding form/field/state/permission and operating schedules by stable version. Actual data/care authority cannot be created merely by selecting a template.

## Schedule A — Facility participation, affiliation and reporting

| Clause | Topic | Draft substantive terms |
|---|---|---|
| LWH-COM-003-A01 | Parties and scope | Identify legal operator/site, Directorate counterparty, ownership category, jurisdictions and exact participating services. Recognition is ministry network affiliation with stated meaning; statutory authority remains separately evidenced. |
| LWH-COM-003-A02 | Standards and evidence | Facility supplies truthful versioned evidence, permits the agreed inspection/review method, declares material changes and follows the adopted criteria and corrective process. Directorate records reasoned final decisions, conditions, validity and review route. |
| LWH-COM-003-A03 | Reporting and quality | Attach adopted forms/indicator/calendar/expected-unit schedule, authorised sources, verification/correction rules and responsible officers. Preserve nil/missing/partial distinctions and source history. Avoid unnecessary identifiable patient reporting. |
| LWH-COM-003-A04 | Publication and service readiness | Define approved public fields, consent/authority, badge meaning, change/withdrawal and readiness updates. A listing does not guarantee capacity or accepted emergency/referral service. |
| LWH-COM-003-A05 | Fees, restriction and continuity | Attach only approved fees and payment terms; payment does not substitute for recognition evidence. Apply proportionate adopted restrictions with notice/review. On exit preserve active care, records custody and historical decisions. |

## Contract control and acceptance

Maintain agreement ID/version, parties/authority, effective/expiry, applicable policies/source licences, amendments, approval and obligations. Legal/privacy/clinical/finance/technical owners review their scopes. Validate that real staffed service commitments, data locations, provider interfaces, consent/custody, offline limitations and financial recognition match the implemented service. Track renewal/termination notices and accepted exit responsibilities. No agreement is represented as executed in this suite.
