# Affiliation, inspection and institutional standards policy

## Recognition and decision rights

Affiliation is ministry network recognition under adopted terms; statutory licensing and professional authority remain separate. Include ministry-owned, member-owned and appropriate external institutions with truthful labels. Final approval, conditional recognition, renewal and adverse decisions are recorded centrally by authorised Directorate decision-makers. Inspectors and regional/zonal staff collect/review/recommend within delegated scope; AI and commercial payment do not confer recognition.

## Criteria and evidence baseline

| Criterion domain | Required evidence / assessment purpose | Owner / method |
|---|---|---|
| Identity/ownership | Operator/site identity, responsible contacts and truthful ownership | Registry steward; private evidence and public label review |
| Applicable statutory/professional authority | Issuing authority, validity and service/credential scope | Qualified local verification; independent from affiliation |
| Clinical governance/staffing | Named clinical lead, competent staff, privileges, coverage and escalation | Staff/service readiness and actual roster |
| Service readiness | Essential equipment, medicines, diagnostics, hours and receiving routes | Observed/checked capability by service |
| Records/privacy/consent | Custody, access, consent, corrections, secure retention and continuity | Document/process evidence and sampled authorised review |
| Medication/diagnostic safety | Approved catalogues, prescription/dispense/MAR, specimen/result verification | Qualified department assessment and workflow tests |
| IPC and environment | Improvement-oriented IPC practices, WASH/environment and safe facility operation | Purpose-appropriate self-assessment/observation; no relabelled WHO audit |
| Patient rights and communication | Accessible information, supported decisions, complaints and non-discrimination | Approved policy, user-facing materials and feedback |
| Reporting and learning | Defined returns/events, responsible officer, data quality and corrective learning | Source verification and reporting capability |
| Referrals/emergency access | Actual capability, contacts, accepted handover and escalation | Tested local operational pathway; no universal dispatch claim |
| Offline and resilience | Local clinical runtime, power/LAN, backup/restore and reconciliation | Qualified outage/restore evidence |
| Improvement and renewal | Owned corrections, review evidence, changed services and reassessment | Conditions/finding closure and renewal dossier |


Adopt a versioned criterion set by facility type, service and country. Define required versus improvement criteria, competent assessor, evidence validity, verification method and finding severity. This is a project-designed baseline informed by sources; it is not full HHFA, official accreditation or a WHO score. The primary-care IPC tool remains a self-assessment/improvement reference according to its stated intended purpose.

## Inspection and decision procedure

Screen application/identity; select applicable criteria; verify source evidence; assign independent competent inspector; record observations versus declarations; notify findings; obtain response/correction; independent reviewer recommends; Directorate records decision, reason, scope, conditions and validity; issue notices; update eligible publication and owned conditions. Preserve originals and conflict declarations. Small teams use a documented independent review arrangement rather than silent self-approval.

## Proposed calendar and review parameters

The leading renewal proposal is one calendar year from the effective decision, consistent with the recording's annual-renewal discussion; exact adoption, expiry handling and jurisdiction constraints require Directorate approval. Proposed reminder offsets are 90/30/7 days before expiry. Submission does not extend validity. Finding/correction/review deadlines are set proportionately by an authorised reviewer based on risk and circumstances, with reasons; no invented universal clinical response time applies.

## Noncompliance, restriction and appeal

Give reasoned notice, relevant evidence, opportunity to respond and an independent review route under adopted terms. Urgent protective restrictions require named authority, evidence, scope/duration and prompt review. Routine reporting lateness cannot automatically erase a chart or abandon patients. Sanctions, publication removal, statutory status and local clinical record access are separately assessed. Reinstatement records corrected evidence and a new decision without deleting the prior history.

## Improvement, publication and continuity

Track accepted corrections and recurrent findings rather than approval volume alone. Public recognition states exact scope/conditions and current dates. Facility withdrawal/closure prompts accepted care/records/referral custody arrangements. Commercial fees, if later adopted, are separately approved and reconciled; standards decisions retain independence. Adopt this policy before any live recognition or adverse action.
