# Operating model, ministry hierarchy and decision rights

## Organisation model

The required ministry spine is GLOBAL_MINISTRY → REGION → ZONE. Optional groups, churches, ministry centres or programme units can be represented through typed, effective-dated relationships after validation. A country is a legal/geographic dimension, not a compulsory intermediate ministry node. A region may span countries; a country may contain multiple regions/zones. Actual names and assignments are imported from the validated ministry master list.

A facility site has an operator/owner and one primary administrative reporting assignment for a reporting period, plus permitted service/referral associations with other zones. This primary assignment is a proposed anti-double-counting rule; changes retain effective dates. Facility ownership is independent of ministry oversight. Network-wide and cross-zone specialist facilities use explicit Directorate-approved assignments rather than duplicate facility records.

The global oversight chain describes administrative responsibility. Clinical access derives from professional role, privileges, treating relationship, subject, purpose and valid authorisation. An executive, region lead or zone coordinator receives no general chart access solely through organisational seniority.

## Role assignments and absence

Assignments specify actor, organisation/facility, role, allowed actions, valid interval, grantor and delegation depth. Privileged administrators use strong authentication. Acting appointments carry expiry and handover obligations. Offboarding revokes sessions and tokens, reassigns open work and preserves authorship. Routine submitter/reviewer separation and finance maker-checker rules apply; small sites use a remote qualified reviewer or a documented approved exception with independent later review.

## Decision and escalation

The Directorate records final affiliation decisions centrally. Regional/zonal teams can coordinate evidence and support within delegated scope; this does not itself confer approval authority. Urgent protective action may temporarily restrict publication or a service under an adopted incident policy, with an identified approver, reason, duration and review. Automated checks can hold an incomplete application; AI cannot grant recognition or impose sanctions.

Reporting problems escalate facility → zone → region → Directorate information lead. Clinical emergencies use the actual local clinical response chain and verified receiving services. Privacy/security and financial incidents use their own accountable leads. A chart note is never disclosed simply to make an administrative escalation easier.

## Governance cadence and accountability

Proposed operating rhythm: daily local clinical/work queues; weekly zone/region exception review; monthly verified institutional reporting and Directorate action review; periodic standards and access reviews. The cadence is a local policy proposal and can vary by indicator/risk. Minutes record decisions and unresolved obligations, not just attendance. Names, contact routes and staffed hours are populated at activation; the system cannot advertise a response commitment without an accepted owner and coverage.
