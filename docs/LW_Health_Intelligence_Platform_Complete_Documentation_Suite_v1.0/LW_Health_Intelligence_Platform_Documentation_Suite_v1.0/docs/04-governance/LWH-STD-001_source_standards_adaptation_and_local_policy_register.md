# Source standards, adaptation and policy register

## Research scope and evidence status

The sources below were checked through official publisher/regulator/specification pages on 5 November 2026, with prior project sources dated separately. Review depth is explicit: some references were reviewed through landing-page/module metadata rather than a complete downloaded clinical instrument. Resource field pages were inspected for selected data semantics. No item-level claim, clinical rule, certification or endorsement is inferred beyond the documented review. The project supplies original workflows/data contracts informed by these domains and the confirmed requirements.

## Source-to-requirement and field adaptation

LWH-SPEC-006 tags every field with a reference domain and original project field ID. That tag does not mean a literal WHO question or FHIR cardinality was copied. The final adopted implementation records exact publisher item/section where applicable, original meaning, local wording/code/unit, additions/omissions, clinical applicability, licence, reviewer, evidence and test. Preserve the Director's existing source-template reconciliation requirement. The currently authored forms are substantive candidate schemas, not placeholders awaiting every field from the ministry.

HL7 EHR-S FM supports capability coverage; FHIR/IPS supports selected exchange structures; WHO DAKs support programme-workflow curation; HHFA supports facility-assessment domains; RHIS/DQA supports reporting interpretation. None replaces organisational decision authority, actual service readiness or national obligations. Versions are pinned to the chosen use case/partner rather than automatically upgraded.

## Licensing and commercial use

Maintain a licence/permission record for every embedded instrument, terminology dataset, software component and content package. WHO publications can carry noncommercial/share-alike terms; the Labour Care Guide metadata explicitly does. Checklist adaptation has attribution/logo conditions. LOINC has its published licence; SNOMED CT access depends on applicable rights. Review commercial permission and downstream redistribution before reproduction. This suite uses original project wording and does not embed WHO logos, complete clinical instruments or proprietary terminology datasets.

## Adoption and maintenance workflow

Source owner selects official version and scope → verifies substance/limitations/licence → maps to project field/rule/metric → clinical/institutional/privacy owner reviews adaptation → tests representative and failure cases → adopts signed applicable package → distributes with version/expiry/rollback → monitors corrections and source changes. Source withdrawal or changed law reopens affected adoption. The raw source evidence and historical packages remain interpretable.

## Open source-depth items

The original Directorate forms and official hierarchy roster remain unavailable. Country-specific laws, clinical privileges, tax/payroll and data-processing decisions remain country-activation work because countries are unselected. The NDPC official material was available as indexed official excerpts, while direct requests were blocked; full operative text/current instruments require qualified review. Some WHO references were checked through publication metadata; exact instrument items and clinical algorithms must be reviewed before embedding/activation. These limits are explicit evidence boundaries, not invented approved content.
