# Country activation, facility privileges and local authority

## Activation policy

Global architecture is approved as product direction; pilot countries remain unselected. Each country/operator/service activation has an evidence dossier with named accountable entities and a bounded decision. Network registration, publication, clinical delivery, record exchange, AI use and optional enterprise modules can have different activation scopes. A commercial contract or a deployed screen does not itself authorise clinical practice.

## Required dossier and sign-off

| Evidence | Responsible reviewer | Required result |
|---|---|---|
| Operator/custodian/contracts | Directorate/legal/privacy | Named entities, purposes, responsibilities, support and exit |
| Medical/software intended use | Qualified local clinical/regulatory reviewer | Classification/obligations and required authorisations assessed for each function |
| Professional and service privileges | Facility clinical director | Actual staff evidence, service/population scope and known restrictions |
| Privacy/records/consent | Privacy/records/local legal | Processing authority, data locations/transfers, retention, rights/representative rules |
| Clinical content/readiness | Clinical/department/network leads | Approved applicable packages, actual staff/equipment/coverage and receiving routes |
| Local runtime/continuity | Facility/platform/security | Qualified WAN-offline operation, backup/restore, authentication and reconciliation |
| Integration/migration | Custodian/interoperability lead | Validated identity/code/unit mapping, actual partner tests and cutover evidence |
| Finance/funding | Directorate/facility finance | Funded operations, approved prices/payer rules, reconciled ledgers and support |
| User adoption | Clinical/product/training owners | Role competence, accessible journeys, handover and incident drills |
| Release evidence | Relevant clinical/privacy/technical/finance/product owners | Exact code/config/content versions, tests, limitations and scoped decision |

## Facility clinical privileges

Privileges bind person, profession, credential issuer/jurisdiction, facility, service/actions, population where relevant, validity, verification and approver. Employment or ministry role does not confer prescribing, surgery, anaesthesia, blood-bank or diagnostic signing rights. Expiry/suspension removes new permitted acts and triggers accepted work transfer. Temporary/locum and delegated local offline authority are explicitly bounded and independently reviewed.

## Local operation and supervision

The facility operator owns real clinical staffing, local infrastructure and safe downtime arrangements. Central Directorate affiliation authority remains central. Local duty authority can manage care and approved provisional/emergency pathways within its adopted scope; it cannot invent professional licences, global sharing rights or central recognition. Knowingly unavailable services are removed from new matching while local records and continuing obligations remain accessible under custody policy.

## Decision, expansion and review

Record country/facility/service, approved populations, data/AI processing scope, dates, cohort/capacity, conditions, owners, evidence and review/expiry. Initial activation can be supervised and capacity-limited based on verified workload. Expansion requires evidence of safe fulfilment, stable reconciliation, trained staff, funding and no unresolved release-blocking issues. Changes in law/source content, new service, processing location, partner or incident reopen the affected assessment. The documentation supplies the dossier/process now; it does not manufacture an approved country package.
