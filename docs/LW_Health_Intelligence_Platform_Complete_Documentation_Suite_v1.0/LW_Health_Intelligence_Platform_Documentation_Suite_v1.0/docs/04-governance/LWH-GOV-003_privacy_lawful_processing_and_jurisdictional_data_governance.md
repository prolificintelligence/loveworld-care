# Privacy, processing purposes and jurisdictional governance

## Purpose and responsible entities

Maintain a processing inventory for account identity, facility affiliation, professional credentials, clinical care, record exchange, institutional reporting, programmes/finance, optional employment/payroll, support and AI. Identify the actual controller/joint-controller/processor/custodian arrangements per purpose and country; product branding or funding alone does not settle those roles. The Directorate's aggregate information needs do not imply central custody of every identifiable chart.

## Data-purpose matrix

| Dataset | Permitted primary purpose | Disclosure baseline |
|---|---|---|
| Public directory | Suitable service discovery | Approved institutional fields only |
| Private affiliation/credential evidence | Standards/privilege assessment | Assigned reviewers and authorised decision-makers |
| Identified clinical record | Care, continuity and applicable obligations | Treating/record roles with valid authority and minimum scope |
| Protected coded case/referral | Authorised review/handover before/full identity disclosure as permitted | Named purpose/recipient; controlled linkage; remains protected |
| Institutional intelligence | Governed leadership/service planning | Aggregate-first and disclosure assessed |
| Programme/financial records | Funding eligibility, service obligations and reconciliation | Contracted financial scope; no unnecessary clinical narratives |
| Optional HR/payroll | Employment/pay administration | Separate restricted roles/storage access |
| AI/support/evaluation | Registered approved task | Minimum authorised data, approved processing location and retention |

## Jurisdiction and lawful processing

No pilot countries are selected. The country dossier establishes applicable health/privacy/software/records duties, processing authority, special-category health-data conditions, children/representatives, rights, transfers, vendor/subprocessor terms, retention, incidents and regulator interfaces. Nigeria NDPC materials are one researched reference, not a universal global rule or a selected pilot. Obtain qualified local review of the operative text and current implementation instruments.

Consent is separated by purpose: treatment decisions, record sharing, optional messages/research and app terms. Document other applicable lawful authority where consent is not the correct basis. Provide understandable notices and supported decisions. Withdrawal stops the future activity governed by that authority while retained lawful clinical records/holds follow the adopted records policy.

## Anonymisation and protected linkage

Anonymous outputs undergo re-identification/disclosure assessment considering rare conditions, geography, dates, combinations and likely available auxiliary information. A reversible/coded patient record is treated as protected and held behind access/purpose controls. Direct identifiers and linkage keys remain separated from analytic projections. Minimum-cohort controls, complementary suppression, restricted dimensions and repeated-query review apply as adopted; a single numeric k threshold is not an anonymity guarantee or formal differential privacy.

## Rights and operations

Provide verified access/copy, correction, sharing withdrawal and complaint workflows with country-specific deadlines and review routes. Minimise data collected and repeated forms. Verify representative authority without disclosing the record first. Support has limited diagnostic data and time-bounded justified access. External messages are neutral by default. Assess privacy impacts before new countries, datasets, AI providers, interfaces, device feeds or secondary use.

## Incident, retention and evidence

Privacy incidents receive containment, preserved evidence, affected-data/recipient assessment, legally required notices and follow-up under the country package. No universal notification deadline is assumed. Retention by record class, legal hold, child/clinical context, backup and vendor exit is adopted in LWH-GOV-004. Maintain the processing inventory, agreements, impact assessments, access reviews, disclosure tests and actual authority records as release evidence. This policy is an authored proposal requiring appropriate institutional/legal adoption.
