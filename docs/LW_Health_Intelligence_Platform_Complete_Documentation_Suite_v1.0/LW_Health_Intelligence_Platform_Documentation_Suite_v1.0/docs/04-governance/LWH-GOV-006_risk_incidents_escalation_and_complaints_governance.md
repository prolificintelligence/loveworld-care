# Risk, incidents, escalation and complaints governance

## Incident classes and responsibility

Clinical safety, privacy/security, technical continuity, financial integrity, standards/affiliation and service complaints have related but distinct accountable leads. One incident coordinator maintains the shared chronology; restricted case evidence stays within the appropriate purpose/role. A technical incident can create clinical obligations and vice versa. Record near misses and uncertain signals without presenting them as confirmed harm.

## Proposed severity and response model

| Class | Trigger examples | Required action |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Imminent/serious patient harm, wrong-patient exposure, invalid clinical signing, lost urgent work, major ledger/stock integrity failure | Immediate local clinical protection/containment, responsible duty lead, evidence preservation and affected-case review |
| High | Material service/data failure with credible harm/exposure risk | Owned containment, safe alternatives, qualified investigation and escalation |
| Moderate | Recoverable task/reporting/interface failure or repeated workflow burden | Assigned correction, deadline and trend review |
| Low/learning | Minor usability issue or improvement signal | Record, prioritise and communicate outcome |

Actual staffed contacts, acknowledgement/review commitments and statutory notices are country/facility settings. The system cannot promise 24-hour coverage or a response time without a real roster. An urgent clinical concern uses the local response chain regardless of whether a support ticket is successfully sent.

## Lifecycle and evidence

Detect/report → acknowledge/triage → protect patients/data/funds → preserve source/audit/configuration → assign investigators → establish facts and affected scope → correct/communicate → verify recovery and reconcile open obligations → review learning/residual risk → close with accountable approval. Notification delivery is not resolution. Technical health restoration does not prove missed results/referrals/payments have been reconciled.

## Complaints and rights

Provide accessible patient/facility/staff complaint routes, minimum context, verified authority for protected data, confidentiality, owner, progress and reasoned resolution/review. Distinguish a complaint, factual correction, clinical incident and legal rights request while linking related cases. Avoid punitive automation from an unverified allegation. Do not publish sensitive narratives in executive learning reports.

## Escalation and communication

Clinical issues escalate through actual duty/service leadership; reporting gaps through facility → zone → region → Directorate information lead; standards through the central secretariat/decider; finance through independent reconciliation authority; privacy/security through their designated leads. Communications say what is known, affected scope, actual limitations and next update. Required external/regulatory notices follow the adopted country package, not a guessed global deadline.

## Review and resume

Independent review considers root/contributing causes, controls, training, workload and recurrence. Publish de-identified learning only after disclosure review. Resume a contained capability after corrective evidence, regression/drill results and approval from relevant clinical/privacy/finance/technical owners. Monitor recurrence and outstanding affected cases after closure. The hazard log and relevant PRDs/specifications/runbooks are updated through controlled change.
