# Interventions, decisions and accountable action

## Purpose and service result

Ensure intelligence results in completed, reviewed work: education, facility support, standards improvement, clinic readiness or referral coordination. Every action has an accepted owner and evidence of its actual result.

**Primary users:** Directorate programme leads, regional/zonal coordinators, facility owners and reviewers. **Accountable product/operating owner:** Directorate programme operations lead. The module is complete only when its downstream responsibility is accepted or its stated final result has evidence. Navigation, loading/empty/stale/error/denied/conflict behaviour and accessibility follow LWH-SPEC-001; entity, form and state definitions are canonical in LWH-SPEC-004/006/009.

## Functional requirements

| Reference | Requirement | Required behaviour | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|
| LWH-PRD-008-R001 | Evidence-linked action | MUST link each intervention to a finding, decision or justified request with scope and intended result. | An action can be traced to the information that prompted it. |
| LWH-PRD-008-R002 | Acceptance | MUST distinguish assigned from accepted responsibility and require a fallback owner. | An unaccepted task remains on the assigning owner exception board. |
| LWH-PRD-008-R003 | Resources | MUST record required resources, budget authority, dependencies and due/review dates. | Unfunded work is visible rather than reported as underway. |
| LWH-PRD-008-R004 | Progress evidence | MUST record milestones, impediments and completion evidence. | An email delivery cannot mark a clinic improvement complete. |
| LWH-PRD-008-R005 | Outcome review | MUST distinguish activity completion from observed outcome and causal claim. | A completed campaign does not automatically claim reduced illness. |
| LWH-PRD-008-R006 | Reopening | MUST support rejected completion, recurrence, owner change and reopening with history. | An overdue action cannot disappear through reassignment. |
| LWH-PRD-008-R007 | Escalation | MUST trigger owned overdue/dependency escalation under adopted calendars. | Reminder failures create an alternative support task. |
| LWH-PRD-008-R008 | Learning | MUST retain lessons, templates and follow-on actions within permitted disclosure. | Sensitive case details do not leak into general learning content. |

## Screen contracts

| Screen | Purpose | Visible content / inputs | Primary actions | Failure and recovery |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LWH-SCR-022 | Action board | Scope, evidence, owner acceptance, due date, resources and blockers | Create, accept, reassign or escalate | No owner/resources is a visible exception |
| LWH-SCR-023 | Intervention workspace | Milestones, support needs, linked education/referral/standards work | Record progress, submit evidence or request assistance | A blocked dependency preserves accountability |
| LWH-SCR-024 | Outcome review | Completion proof, reviewer findings, subsequent measures and limitations | Accept, reject, reopen or schedule review | Missing evidence prevents final acceptance |

Every screen displays the active facility/subject or administrative scope. Destructive or consequential actions require the applicable role and server-side state guard; hiding a control is insufficient. Dates show the relevant local time zone. Reuse previously verified information with a freshness check instead of repeating onboarding.

## End-to-end journey

A reviewed concern creates an intervention proposal. The programme owner approves scope/resources, a delivery owner accepts and coordinates local tasks. Evidence is submitted, an independent reviewer accepts completion and a later outcome review considers measures and limitations. Unresolved issues create reopened or follow-on work.

## Operational responsibilities and exceptions

Programme owners own outcomes and resources; delivery owners own execution; coordinators resolve cross-unit dependencies; reviewers examine evidence. Clinical work generated by an intervention still needs its own clinical authority and individual care ownership.

## Offline and integration behaviour

Local progress/evidence capture persists and queues. Cross-network assignments remain pending until the responsible node/person acknowledges. Resource approvals requiring central authority are not invented locally.

## Acceptance cases

| Reference | Scenario | Exercise | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| LWH-PRD-008-AT001 | Unaccepted owner | Assign an intervention to an absent coordinator. | Escalation retains an accountable originator and no false acceptance. |
| LWH-PRD-008-AT002 | Completion proof | Mark work complete without required evidence. | Review blocks closure and identifies missing proof. |
| LWH-PRD-008-AT003 | Outcome limitation | Complete education and compare later reports with changed coverage. | System shows coverage differences and avoids unsupported causal claims. |

These cases are specifications, not executed tests. LWH-ENG-003/004 add cross-module, permission, concurrency and operational evidence. Release requires the exact deployed configuration, staff readiness and relevant country/facility approval.

## Measures and build handoff

Track acceptance lag, overdue/unfunded actions, evidence rejection, completion and reviewed outcomes. Definitions, coverage and exclusions are controlled in LWH-SPEC-007. The feature ticket references the requirement IDs above, canonical data/forms/states/APIs, owner, authority node, error recovery, telemetry and acceptance evidence. Module-specific defects must be resolved rather than hidden by an aggregate task-success score.
