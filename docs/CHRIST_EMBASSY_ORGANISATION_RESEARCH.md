# Christ Embassy Region → Zone research

Researched 5 November 2026 at the project lead's request. Public ministry and zonal sources supply useful evidence, but the research did not locate a complete, current, centrally issued global master register with stable identifiers and effective dates.

The [Dallas Zone site](https://christembassydallaszone.org/) identifies USA Region 2 and CE Dallas Zone. The [New York site](https://www.christembassynewyork.org/) is indexed as USA Region 1 Zone 2; direct retrieval timed out, so this has a lower verification level. A [central ministry article](https://christembassy.org/pastor-chris-reveals-amazing-truths-during-q-a-segment-at-global-service/) explicitly names UK Region 2, Zone 4, but it is historical rather than a current master-list release.

The [Lagos Zone 1 directory](https://celz1.org/contact-us/church-locations/), [Lagos Zone 2](https://www.celz2.org/rhapsody-signup.php), [Lagos Zone 5](https://www.celz5.org/index.php/home), [Lagos Zone 6](https://live.christembassycitychurch.com/) and [central ministry's Port Harcourt Zone 1 form](https://www.christembassy.org/blwcommunity/?zone=Port+Harcourt+Zone+1) provide zone identity evidence. They do not establish the missing regional parents. Geographic proximity does not establish ministry reporting authority.

The machine-readable evidence is in `services/api/src/lw_care_api/platform/organisation_sources.json`, accessible to authenticated staff through the Organisation workspace. It records source URL, evidence level and limitations for every finding. These records are research evidence, not seeded organisational authorities or invented ministry entities. A Directorate administrator records the adopted names, registry codes, parent relationship and effective dates before real reporting uses them.

No people, credentials, health facilities, approved affiliation criteria or treatment rules are derived from these church directories.
